The process

Four steps. Nothing in between.

  1. 01 Source Carob pods grown 20 minutes from our kitchen, in northern New South Wales. Same farmer every season. We pick when the pods are leather-dark and snap when bent.
  2. 02 Roast Pods are roasted low and slow until the natural sugars caramelise. We taste at three points and stop when the carob smells like burnt honey, before any bitterness develops.
  3. 03 Press Roasted carob is ground fine and pressed with cacao butter. Three or four ingredients per bar, no emulsifier, no vegetable oil, no rounding the corners on flavour.
  4. 04 Pack Tempered, scored, packed by hand. Every bar is touched by one of us before it leaves the room. Small batch is a description, not a marketing line.

Carli runs the creative, the formulation, the family. Dylan runs the production, the equipment, the manufacturing relationships. Between us we taste every batch before it ships.
