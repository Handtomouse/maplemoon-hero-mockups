From the pod

What is carob?

Carob is a pod from a Mediterranean tree, roasted and ground. It is naturally sweet, so it needs no added sugar. No caffeine, no theobromine, nothing bitter to engineer around. We grow ours in Northern New South Wales and press it with cacao butter, down the road from Byron Bay.
