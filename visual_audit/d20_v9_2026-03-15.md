# D20 Design Review — V9 Story Cards
**File:** ~/maplemoon-website/hero_v9.html
**Date:** 2026-03-15
**Reviewer:** Claude Code (automated via Responsive Viewer + code audit)
**Breakpoints reviewed:** iPhone 390px | iPad 768px | Desktop 1440px

---

## Brand Rules (non-negotiable)
- No inline colour values — use CSS vars from brand_kit.css only
- No em dashes, no "vibe" in copy
- No bold on hero text unless structural need
- All a11y preserved: focus trap, aria labels, reduced-motion guards
- CSS vars: --mm-cream, --mm-navy, --mm-carob-dark, --mm-serif, --mm-sans

---

## Issues

### [1][VISUAL][P1] iPhone: card text invisible on Card 1
Card 1 (Who: MapleMoon.) text is not visible on iPhone. The warm dark carob pods photo absorbs cream text even with the rgba(58,42,26,0.4) overlay. Text-shadow is present but insufficient.
**Fix:** Increase overlay opacity on card-pod to 0.55. On mobile (<768px), override .card-pod .card-content to `position: absolute; bottom: 80px; left: 32px; right: 32px; text-align: left;` to anchor text out of the centre-blend dead zone.

### [2][LAYOUT][P1] iPad: 4 narrow panels at 768px is too cramped
At 768px, the 4-panel accordion fires at 25vw each = ~192px columns. The card-label (2.6rem) wraps or overflows. Cards feel squashed and unreadable.
**Fix:** Delay the 4-panel accordion to `min-width: 1024px`. At 768px-1023px (tablet), keep the mobile swipe/snap behaviour. Update the JS dot script breakpoint to match.

### [3][VISUAL][P1] Card 3 (What): packaging text visible through overlay
The carob bar hand photo shows the MapleMoon packaging with "pure carob & cacao butter / maple moon" clearly readable through the rgba(30,20,10,0.35) overlay. This text competes directly with the "What:" card label.
**Fix:** Increase card-bar overlay opacity to 0.58. Also adjust `background-position` to `center bottom` so the hand + bar fills the lower frame and the packaging label area is obscured by the crop.

### [4][VISUAL][P2] Adobe Stock watermarks on Cards 1 and 2
Cards 1 (carob pods macro) and 2 (Byron Bay lighthouse) use Adobe Stock preview images with visible diagonal watermarks. These cannot ship.
**Fix:** 🔴 REVIEW — Nate to license AdobeStock_245325729 and AdobeStock_300794943, or source replacements. Flag filenames: assets/hero_shots/hero_carob_pods_macro.jpg and hero_byron_lighthouse.jpg.

### [5][TYPOGRAPHY][P2] card-label font-size inverted at desktop vs mobile
card-label is 3.6rem on mobile (full-width card) and 2.6rem on desktop (25vw accordion). At the collapsed 20vw hover state on desktop, the label may wrap.
**Fix:** Keep 2.6rem at resting 25vw. Add `.story-track:hover .story-card:not(:hover) .card-label { font-size: 1.4rem; }` for collapsed cards. Use transition: font-size 0.5s to match width transition timing.

### [6][VISUAL][P2] Overlay strength inconsistent across 4 cards
- Card 1 (pods): 0.40 — warm brown
- Card 2 (lighthouse): 0.45 — cool navy
- Card 3 (bar): 0.35 — darkest brown, busiest photo, weakest overlay
- Card 4 (moons): 0.40 — cool navy
**Fix:** Normalise to Card 1 → 0.50, Card 2 → 0.40, Card 3 → 0.58, Card 4 → 0.45.

### [7][VISUAL][P2] Progress dots: only 3 visible on iPhone
In Responsive Viewer screenshot, only 3 of 4 progress dots are visible. Possible cause: total dot width + gap clips the 4th dot outside the viewport on narrow screens.
**Fix:** Verify `.progress-dots` CSS. If total width (4×8px dots + 3×10px gaps = 62px) is clipping, reduce dot size to 6px or gap to 7px. Test at 375px and 390px.

### [8][COPY][P2] Card 2 subtitle is product copy, not place copy
"Organic. Vegan. Caffeine free." belongs on a product card. Card 2 is the Byron Bay/craft card.
**Fix:** Change Card 2 subtitle from "Organic. Vegan. Caffeine free." to "Handcrafted in small batches."

### [9][MOTION][P3] No entrance animation on any card
All other hero variants use .fade-1/.fade-2 stagger from brand_kit.css. V9 has no entrance animation — content appears instantly on load.
**Fix:** Add class="fade-1" to .card-label and class="fade-2" to .card-answer on Card 1. On desktop, add a JS class toggle on card hover so card-label and card-answer animate in on expand (add/remove 'is-revealed' class, trigger CSS animation).

### [10][LAYOUT][P3] Card content vertically centred on mobile — text lost in mid-photo
All 4 cards use flex justify-content: center, placing text at the photo midpoint. On dark full-bleed photos, centred text blends in.
**Fix:** On mobile (<768px), push text to lower third: add `margin-top: auto; padding-bottom: 80px;` to .card-content so text sits near the bottom where contrast is higher and breathing room exists above.

### [11][COPY][P3] Card 4 subtitle tier missing
Cards 1, 2, 3 have a 3-tier label / answer / subtitle hierarchy. Card 4 only has label / answer / CTA. The missing tier makes the card feel incomplete vs the set.
**Fix:** Add `<p class="card-subtitle">Shop the full range.</p>` to Card 4 between .card-answer and .cta-btn.

### [12][VISUAL][P3] Swipe hint low contrast on dark pods photo
The swipe hint "Swipe →" has opacity: 0.6 on the very dark carob pods Card 1. Contrast may fall below readable threshold.
**Fix:** Add a pill background to the swipe-hint: `background: rgba(0,0,0,0.25); border-radius: 20px; padding: 4px 12px;`

### [13][LAYOUT][P3] Text spacing inconsistency — flex gap vs manual margin-top
Cards 1 and 2 use block layout with margin-top on text children. Cards 3 and 4 use flex gap. Results in visually different spacing between label / answer / subtitle across the 4 cards.
**Fix:** Standardise all 4 .card-X .card-content to `display: flex; flex-direction: column; align-items: center; gap: 8px;`. Remove all margin-top from .card-answer and .card-subtitle elements.

### [14][VISUAL][P3] Desktop hover collapse — card-label wraps at 20vw
When a card is collapsed (20vw on hover), the 2.6rem card-label "Where:" may wrap to two lines on smaller desktop screens (1024px-1280px).
**Fix:** `.story-track:hover .story-card:not(:hover) .card-label { font-size: 1.4rem; }` with `transition: font-size 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`.

### [15][VISUAL][P3] No affordance on desktop that cards are hover-expandable
Desktop shows 4 equal columns with cursor: pointer but no visual cue that hovering expands them. First-time users have no signal.
**Fix:** Add a per-card subtle expand indicator — a small centered chevron `›` absolutely positioned at bottom-center, opacity 0.4, that fades to opacity: 0 on hover. CSS-only, no JS required.
