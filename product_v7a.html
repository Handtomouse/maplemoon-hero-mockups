What is carob?

Carob is a naturally sweet pod grown on trees across Australia and the Mediterranean. Unlike cacao, it contains zero caffeine and requires no added sugar. It's roasted, milled, and tempered into bars, just like chocolate, but with a flavour entirely its own.

The Original Pure Carob bar is MapleMoon in its simplest form. Australian-grown carob, slow-roasted and milled, combined with organic cacao butter and hand-tempered in our Byron Bay kitchen.

There's nothing added because nothing needs to be. Carob is naturally sweet, rich in flavour, and entirely free from caffeine. Each 80g bar is wrapped by hand and made in small batches.

If you've never tried carob before, start here.
