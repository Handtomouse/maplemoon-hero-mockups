What is carob?

Carob is a naturally sweet pod that grows in long, dark seedpods on the carob tree. We roast and stone-mill it, then fold it into bars with simple, real ingredients.

No caffeine, no theobromine, nothing added. Just the honest sweetness of the pod itself.
