## Minimum receipt

For each approved quote, retain a written message, email, form response, or equivalent record that identifies the reviewer and the exact wording and attribution approved. If wording is edited later, obtain approval for the revised wording too.

Until every required field is confirmed, keep the external review candidate on neutral placeholders. Do not add names or quotes to the hosted candidate based only on verbal recollection.
