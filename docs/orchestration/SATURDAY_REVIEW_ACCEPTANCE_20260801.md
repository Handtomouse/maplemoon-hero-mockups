# MapleMoon Saturday Review Acceptance — 1 August 2026

## Deliverable

A polished, evidence-backed six-page front-end review package:

- Homepage
- Carob Story
- Shop
- Our Story
- Stockists
- FAQ

It is not a finished Shopify store, production launch or commerce cutover.

## Audiences

Two surfaces must be produced from the same approved page state:

1. **Annotated Carli/coordinator review:** coloured section status, decision notes and explicit requests.
2. **Clean family-and-friends preview:** no internal markers, WIP labels, unresolved controls or visible placeholders.

Both surfaces must be non-indexed. Their actual page content must match. Access and delivery require separate verification and approval.

## Quality threshold

Saturday fails while any critical or major issue remains in the included package.

Minor polish may carry only when an ordinary reviewer would not notice it or interpret the site as unfinished. Every carried item needs an owner and later gate.

Never classify these as minor:

- unsupported, disputed or incorrect public copy;
- incorrect product, price, size, availability or selling information;
- missing content or assets needed to understand the page;
- broken navigation, links, controls, forms or media;
- horizontal overflow, clipping or unreadable responsive layout;
- keyboard traps, poor contrast or undersized tap targets;
- privacy, consent, indexing or access failures;
- visible placeholders, pending labels or disabled-looking production controls on the clean surface.

## Required evidence

### Presentation

- current versus proposed evidence for every subjective change;
- 1440, 1024, 430, 390, 375 and 320px checks;
- desktop/mobile focal hierarchy and image crop review;
- no horizontal overflow, clipped text or accidental card/seam treatment;
- coherent shared navigation, footer, type, spacing, colour and controls.

### Accessibility and interaction

- keyboard-only path;
- visible focus;
- 200% zoom;
- tap targets;
- reduced motion;
- semantic heading and landmark review;
- accordion, filters, search and navigation behaviour.

### Content and trust

- every current Carli note mapped to affected page occurrences;
- exact public replacement copy approved before application;
- placeholders, consent-held testimonials and missing assets excluded from the clean surface;
- WooCommerce remains the operational product/pricing comparator until catalogue gates pass;
- no page presents a blocked commerce or policy assumption as confirmed.

### Technical and delivery

- all internal destinations, anchors and local assets resolve;
- noindex metadata and canonical handling are correct for both review surfaces;
- annotated and clean packages are generated from one approved source state;
- asset allowlist/manifest is current;
- browser/device smoke checks are recorded;
- image/video weight and obvious performance regressions are checked;
- exact frozen version, hashes, reviewer audience and feedback route are recorded.

## Feedback and ownership

All feedback enters one coordinator-owned register tagged by reviewer, page, section, severity, evidence, decision owner and state. Reviewers do not edit source files. Duplicate or conflicting feedback is reconciled before implementation.

Nate retains final authority for subjective design, ambiguous copy, catalogue/pricing, visible placeholders, Saturday inclusion/defer decisions, staging audience, client/family/friend sends, git, deployment, Shopify, production and final acceptance.

## Agent-team capacity

- three disjoint read-only reviewers may prepare evidence concurrently;
- one mutating worker per page/file cluster;
- no overlapping writers or shared-file mutation;
- every mutation is followed by independent verification;
- page implementation order: Homepage → Carob Story → Shop → Our Story → Stockists → FAQ.

## Current hard gates

- The review package intentionally permits Adobe Typekit only. Final runtime evidence must show no external host outside the Typekit allowlist, and the artifact must be described as networked review rather than zero-network.
- Catalogue acceptance remains blocked until the fresh WooCommerce export and approved retail catalogue exist and pass provenance/schema/PII checks.
- Carli's claim corrections require exact approved replacement wording before WIP application.
- Consent-held testimonials cannot appear on a clean externally shared surface.

## Resolved clean-review behavior

- ordinary clean and annotated routes expose the existing header cart and a local review drawer;
- the 20 non-pending rendered product cards say Add to cart; Pure Carob Moon and Cayenne Moon are hidden from clean and unavailable in annotated;
- cart disclosure appears inside the selection and checkout journey, which collects no order, payment or personal information;
- unresolved or unsupported material is omitted from ordinary clean review and retained in annotated evidence;
- newsletter forms simulate receipt locally, discard the entered address and clearly say it was not saved;
- stockist wording uses Nate's approved rounded 200+ count from the 204-entry working directory while incomplete records remain excluded;
- the clean Homepage stockist mini-finder carries store, suburb, postcode or state queries into confirmed full-directory results without exposing provisional cards or map behavior;
- the cart may repeat facts already displayed on the derived review cards, but authoritative product, price, availability and selling-option acceptance remains blocked until CAT-01 and VIS-02B2.

## Acceptance statement

The Saturday package is accepted only when every included section is either `locked` or intentionally invisible/deferred, all critical and major checks pass, the clean surface looks deliberate to an ordinary reviewer, and Nate approves the exact frozen artifact and audience.
