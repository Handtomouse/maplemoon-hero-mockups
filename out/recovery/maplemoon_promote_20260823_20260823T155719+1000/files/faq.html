What is carob?

Carob comes from the pod of the carob tree (Ceratonia siliqua), which grows across the Mediterranean and in parts of Australia. The pods are harvested, roasted, and milled into a powder that's naturally sweet, no sugar required. We combine it with organic cacao butter to make our bars, moons, bites, and elixirs.

Does it taste like chocolate?

Carob has its own distinct flavour. It's rich and naturally sweet with warm, toasty notes. Some people find it reminiscent of chocolate, others find it completely different. We'd rather you try it and decide for yourself. It's not trying to be chocolate. It's carob.

Is MapleMoon vegan?

Yes. Every product in our range is 100% vegan. We use organic cacao butter as our fat base, never dairy, never compound. Our kitchen is entirely plant-based.

Is it organic and gluten-free?

Yes to both. All MapleMoon products are certified organic and gluten-free. They're also caffeine-free and contain no refined sugar.

Where do you source your carob?

We use Australian-grown carob that's sun-ripened and harvested locally. The pods are slow-roasted and milled in small batches.

How should I store MapleMoon products?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Carob and cacao butter can soften in warm conditions. If your bars arrive soft during summer, a few minutes in the fridge will restore the snap. Carob Powder should be kept sealed in a cool, dry cupboard.

Do you ship Australia-wide?

Yes. We ship to all Australian addresses. Standard shipping is $16.95, and orders over $99 ship free. Orders are packed and dispatched within 1-2 business days.

What is your returns policy?

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 7 days at info@maplemoon.com.au and we'll make it right. Due to the nature of food products, we cannot accept returns on opened items.

What about allergens?

MapleMoon products are made in a facility that processes tree nuts (hazelnuts, coconut). All products are gluten-free, dairy-free, and soy-free. Specific allergen information is listed on each product page and on the packaging. If you have a severe allergy, please contact us before ordering.
