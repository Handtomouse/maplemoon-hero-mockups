# Shot list and call sheet

Project ID: maplemoon_carob_story_pickup  
Primary crew: Mitch West and Maelee Fauchet, Enlighten Media  
Required images: five  
Optional image: one carob-branch hero  
Primary delivery world: real grove, real specimens, diffuse natural light

## Call sheet dependencies

Do not set the date until these are physically available:

- Access to a real carob orchard with a clean row/grove view
- A real branch carrying two to three ripe, dried pods with complete silhouettes
- One separate verified mature dried carob pod for the comparison
- One verified real mature cacao specimen, with source receipt
- A clean knife and food-safe board if the cacao must be opened on set
- One sealed and labelled batch of the exact roasted carob material the page means by The roast
- Written Maple Moon or process-owner confirmation of that batch and stage
- Matte warm-grey or limestone board, target #e6e3dc
- Gray cards and ColorChecker
- Physical slate

## Light and camera

- Stable overcast or open shade
- Portable diffusion above and to front-left, apparent key direction 30 to 45 degrees
- White bounce only; no coloured fill
- Key-to-fill ratio 1.5:1 to 2:1
- Measured white balance 5200K to 5800K
- Stop when direct sun appears, CCT leaves tolerance, or incident exposure changes by more than one third stop
- One full-frame body, at least 24MP and 6000 x 4000
- 14-bit RAW plus camera JPEG
- Tripod and tethered review
- ISO 64 to 100
- Manual exposure and manual focus
- No in-camera HDR, scene mode, beauty processing or computational replacement
- Prefer one 50mm macro or normal prime for the five selected frames; a wider lens may be used for the grove only if recorded
- f/8 for specimen, pod and roast; f/5.6 to f/8 for grove
- Vary shutter speed rather than aperture or ISO when light changes within tolerance
- Capture ColorChecker and gray card at the beginning and end of every setup

## Required framing overlays

Load overlays for:

- Comparison live ratio at 390: 0.545
- Comparison live ratio at 834: 1.213
- Comparison live ratio at 1440: 1.790
- Gallery delivery ratio: 1.430
- Optional hero: 1.307, 2.898 and 1.437

The comparison pair is the hardest responsive crop. Use the exact same locked 4:3 master framing for carob and cacao:

- Complete specimen and stem inside x=30 to 70 percent and y=13 to 87 percent
- Preferred silhouette box 28 to 32 percent of master width and 55 to 62 percent of master height
- Clear matte field surrounding the specimen
- Also capture matched portrait 2:3 and landscape 16:9 insurance frames without changing reproduction scale, camera height or light

## Shot 1, cmp_carob

Purpose: left side of the factual carob versus cacao comparison.

- One real, mature, dried carob pod
- Complete tip, stem and silhouette visible
- Show the real long, flat, gently curved body, longitudinal ridges, thickness, wear and colour
- No pile, extra pod, garnish, loose leaves or decorative styling
- Do not bend or arrange the pod into a moon or crescent
- Use the locked comparison set
- Capture vertical-friendly and horizontal-friendly orientation as separate, clearly logged setups
- Keep the HTML label area visually quiet

Identity slate:

- Specimen source
- Variety if known
- Harvest/ripeness state
- Supplier or tree/row ID
- Name of person confirming it

## Shot 2, cmp_cacao

Purpose: right side of the same factual comparison.

- One verified real mature cacao specimen
- Use a clean opened half only if pulp and beans are the intended fact to show
- Keep the true shell, pulp and seed placement
- Do not remove damaged seeds or make the anatomy more regular
- State explicitly that it is comparison material and not grown at the carob farm
- Swap it into the locked carob setup without moving camera, tripod, board or lights
- Match apparent scale, centroid, breathing room, baseline and shadow to cmp_carob
- Capture the same vertical-friendly and horizontal-friendly orientations

Identity slate:

- Supplier
- Purchase/source receipt ID
- Species/variety if known
- Date opened
- Name of person confirming it

## Shot 3, gallery_grove

Purpose: numbered step 1, The grove.

- Real carob orchard row with one clear vanishing point
- Real ground and tree context
- Diffuse sky with recoverable highlights
- No people, vehicles, signs, powerlines or property clutter
- Eye or chest-height camera
- Level verticals
- Medium to deep focus
- Compose directly for 1.430
- Preserve at least 10 percent final-frame clearance around essential tree and horizon detail

This shot may use a wider lens than the still life, but must use the same camera body, profile, date, weather and recorded light family.

## Shot 4, gallery_pod

Purpose: numbered step 2, The pod.

- One clear cluster of two to three ripe dried pods, naturally attached to a real branch
- Complete silhouettes with stem attachment, ridges, thickness and natural edge wear
- At least 10 percent final-frame clearance
- Soft orchard context only, never a dense hanging curtain
- No loose decorative pods and no rearrangement of the branch
- Compose directly for 1.430

If no suitable complete cluster exists on the day, hold the shot. Do not rebuild one from separate branches or pods.

## Shot 5, gallery_roast

Purpose: numbered step 3, The roast.

- One genuine, unambiguous roast stage identified by the process owner
- One shallow matte vessel or one simple mound only
- Slightly above, about 20 to 30 degrees
- Raking diffuse light sufficient to show real particle or process texture
- No other powder tones
- No syrup, seeds, kibble, raw pods, spoons, garnish, labels or extra bowls
- Compose directly for 1.430

The physical slate must name:

- Exact product or material
- Batch ID
- Roast stage/name
- Roast date if known
- Name and signature of the Maple Moon or process owner confirming the caption truth

If The roast refers to an active roasting action rather than finished powder, photograph that genuine process instead. Do not fake smoke, heat, equipment or a stage that did not occur.

## Optional shot, hero_branch

Quote separately.

- A real compact branch carrying three to five ripe pods
- Choose an existing branch; do not move or invent pods
- Pale, quiet documentary background
- Anchor morphology within x=20 to 80 percent and y=30 to 70 percent
- Capture dedicated 4:3 mobile, 3:1 tablet and 3:2 desktop framings
- Do not rely on one ordinary 3:2 frame to survive the live 2.898 tablet crop

## Physical slate

First frame of every setup:

- maplemoon_carob_story_pickup
- Local date and time with timezone
- Photographer legal name
- Shot ID and setup ID
- Farm/location
- Camera and lens serials
- Weather, CCT and incident-light reading
- Surface and backdrop IDs
- Specimen/batch ID
- Rights owner and property contact

## Capture order

1. Access, rights and weather slate
2. Grove while the diffuse window is stable
3. Pod on tree
4. Roast on the portable board at grove edge
5. Comparison carob
6. Comparison cacao without breaking the locked setup
7. Optional hero
8. Final ColorChecker, gray card and light reading

Review each shot tethered under its exact viewport masks before breaking the setup. If the light leaves tolerance, start the affected setup again with a new slate rather than mixing frames.

## Minimum capture coverage

Per required shot:

- 12 clean frames
- Three focus-confirmation frames per required composition
- Normal exposure plus small safety bracket
- Blank plate from the same locked setup
- Gray-card and ColorChecker frames
- At least one camera JPEG for immediate review

Focus stacking or exposure blending is permitted only from logged, locked frames with no subject movement and no blend seam crossing botanical or powder anatomy.
