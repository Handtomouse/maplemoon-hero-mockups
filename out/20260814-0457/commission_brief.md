# MapleMoon carob story controlled pickup

Prepared for: Enlighten Media, Mitch West and Maelee Fauchet  
Commissioning contact: Nate Don, HandToMouse Studio  
Client: Maple Moon  
Status: request for availability and quote, not a booking

## The job

We need one tightly controlled pickup shoot for Maple Moon's carob story page. The current photographs are real but cannot be made into a coherent set because they came from different years, cameras and lighting conditions.

The pickup is five required images, made by one photographer on one local calendar day under one stable diffuse-light family:

1. Carob comparison specimen
2. Cacao comparison specimen
3. Real carob grove
4. Real ripe pod on the tree
5. One unambiguous real roast

Please quote the optional carob-branch hero separately.

The aim is not a large campaign or a decorative lifestyle shoot. This is factual, quiet, documentary product and botanical photography. Shape truth and matched light matter more than styling.

## Brand world

- Pale blue, cream and warm natural carob tones
- Calm, generous negative space
- Soft directional light and organic shadow
- Muted saturation and restrained contrast
- Real textures, real specimens and real process
- Premium but handmade, never supermarket-polished

Do not use a blue paper set for this pickup. The previous blue generated studies are specifically rejected. Use a matte warm-grey or limestone neutral around #e6e3dc, with real orchard context falling softly out of focus where practical.

## Non-negotiables

- One named photographer, one day, one location/farm and one camera body for the selected set
- Stable overcast or open shade, reinforced by one portable diffusion and white-bounce setup
- No direct sun, on-camera flash, hard specular light or mixed-colour sources
- No generative fill, content-aware reconstruction, synthetic relighting or anatomy cleanup
- Do not add, remove, move, clone or reshape pods, stems, seeds, pulp, scars, ridges or powder particles
- No garnish, ingredient scatter, decorative pods or moon/crescent arrangements
- The carob and cacao images are made in one locked camera, tripod, background and lighting setup
- The roast must show one real, client-verified roast state, not an assortment of powders or props
- If the real roast cannot be documented truthfully, the shot is held rather than staged

## Production model

Preferred location is at or beside the real carob grove, subject to farm access. Build the specimen still-life set at the grove edge so the comparison, pod and roast inherit the same daylight and environmental palette.

Suggested crew:

- Mitch: lead photographer, lighting and capture
- Maelee: set, specimen handling and digital support
- Australian Carob Co representative: farm access and carob identification
- Maple Moon representative: roast-batch and caption truth signoff
- Nate: remote or on-set art direction and crop approval

## Photographer deliverables

- Untouched 14-bit RAW originals and camera JPEGs
- Full EXIF/IPTC/XMP preserved
- Gray-card and ColorChecker frames for every setup
- At least 12 clean frames per required shot
- Full-resolution neutral sRGB selects
- One contact sheet containing slates, RAW proxies and selected frames
- Capture manifest and SHA-256 checksums
- Written location/property permission
- Cacao source receipt and botanical identification
- Roast batch/process confirmation signed by the process owner
- Written photographer usage grant covering website, editorial, ecommerce, social, paid promotion, crops, retouching and derivative layouts
- Credit requirement or written credit waiver

HandToMouse will make the final shared grade, responsive crops and page contact sheet unless post-production is separately quoted and accepted.
