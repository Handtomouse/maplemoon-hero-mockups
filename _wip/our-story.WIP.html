Our story

Carob, on its own terms.

Naturally sweet, caffeine free, and grown right here in Australia. We make carob because we love what it is, not what it stands in for.

01Why carob

It started with a question: why is carob treated as a substitute?

Carob isn't trying to be chocolate. It's its own thing. Naturally sweet, caffeine free, and grown right here in Australia. We believed carob deserved better than the back shelf. Better ingredients, better craft, better packaging. Something you'd reach for because you wanted it, not because you were avoiding something else.

Australian-grown carob pods, sun-ripened and slow-roasted. That's the starting point.

Everything we make comes back to the pod.

02The craft

Made by hand, batch by batch.

The carob is roasted low and slow to bring out its natural sweetness, then milled with cacao butter. Each batch is hand-tempered, hand-moulded and hand-packed. The process is slower than it needs to be. We prefer it that way.

03The place

From Brunswick Heads, far north coast NSW.

Maple Moon began in Brunswick Heads, a small river town on the far north coast of New South Wales. It is the home our carob range grew from, and a bit of this place travels with every bar.

Staying small keeps everything in our hands, from the first roast to the last fold of the wrapper.

The makers

Carli & Dylan

Maple Moon is Carli and Dylan. Together they make every carob bar in small batches from Australian-grown carob, milled with cacao butter.

Small batches, made by two pairs of hands.
