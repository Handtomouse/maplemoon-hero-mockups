# Hero Middle Band Review - 2026-07-19

Scope: above-fold hero through first product range reveal.

Design target: keep the moonlit ocean and fog blending down into the range, then give each middle-layer element a clear job:

1. Hero claim: brand, carob wordmark, tagline, primary CTA.
2. Proof: quiet factual strip.
3. Range navigation: categories as shopping controls.
4. Range reveal: title, product scale, carousel controls.

Use existing copy and assets only. Avoid fake claims, fake reviews, pricing changes, bananas, and visible em dashes.

## System-Level Issues

1. The strongest current direction is the continuous moonlit background from the reference screenshot.
2. Hard cream shelves make the hero feel split into unrelated sections.
3. The middle band has too many competing focal points in the same vertical zone.
4. Category navigation sometimes looks decorative instead of actionable.
5. Proof claims and category tabs are visually similar, so the user has to parse too much.
6. The product title occasionally collides with the dark-to-light transition.
7. Product pack scale needs to feel anchored to a shelf, not floating.
8. Mobile needs a designed order, not a compressed desktop.
9. Board labels should be short. Long rationale belongs in this review file.
10. A single signature move should carry the system: the moonpath blend.

## R1 - Huge CAROB and Icon Arc

1. Keep the huge CAROB scale, but move it slightly higher to clear the bridge band.
2. Retain the cinematic ocean image as the primary background.
3. Extend the background down behind the range reveal with a mist layer.
4. Replace the low arc with a flatter moonpath dock so it does not collide with the range title.
5. Keep category icons, but treat them as controls with larger hit areas.
6. Separate proof copy from category navigation with vertical spacing.
7. Reduce category label letter spacing for readability.
8. Use one active category underline, not multiple glow cues.
9. Move the proof strip closer to CTAs, because it supports the purchase action.
10. Put the range title below category navigation, not underneath the arc.
11. Use a faint horizon rule only where it reinforces the ocean path.
12. Make the product row start lower and larger so it feels like a reveal.
13. Add desktop carousel arrows outside the product pack group.
14. Use the icon arc only as a reference if it helps flow. Do not force the curve.
15. On mobile, replace the arc with a horizontally scrollable dock.
16. On mobile, keep proof as two rows maximum.
17. On mobile, let the product range peek before the fold.
18. Avoid translucent text over high-contrast water shimmer.
19. Avoid letting the range title sit half on night and half on fog.
20. Recommended use: cinematic hero with a simpler bridge dock.

## R2 - Moon Medallion and Product Shelf

1. Keep the moon medallion, but reduce its size.
2. Place the medallion between hero and bridge as a small ritual marker, not a second headline.
3. Use the medallion to guide the eye into the moonpath blend.
4. Remove the hard cream curve.
5. Use a soft mist ramp from navy to pale blue instead of a cream shelf.
6. Keep the huge CAROB, but do not let the medallion push CTAs too low.
7. Put CTAs before the medallion on mobile to preserve conversion.
8. Make the category dock a separate light band with enough breathing room.
9. Keep category labels short on mobile: Bars, Crescents, Elixirs, Bites.
10. Keep full category names on desktop if there is room.
11. Place proof as small facts under the CTAs, not inside the dock.
12. Give the active category a clear underline and icon tint.
13. Add carousel arrows only in the product zone.
14. Make the product row feel centered on the moon reflection.
15. Increase lower background brightness behind products for package readability.
16. Avoid text over the cream curve edge because it produces ghosting.
17. Use no more than one circular motif in the bridge.
18. On iPhone, show the medallion as a compact divider.
19. On iPhone, avoid a pill dock wider than the viewport.
20. Recommended use: highest ROI if paired with the continuous background blend.

## R3 - Glass Panel and Proof Grid

1. Keep the panel idea only if it provides structure.
2. Reduce the glass panel height so it does not trap the whole hero.
3. Let the background remain visible outside the panel.
4. Make the panel feel like atmosphere, not a card sitting on top.
5. Keep proof icons inside the panel, but reduce their visual weight.
6. Move category navigation outside the panel so it remains a shopping control.
7. Avoid duplicating icon systems in proof and categories at the same scale.
8. Make the divider beneath CAROB shorter and quieter.
9. Use the panel to frame the main message only.
10. Keep CTAs visible and high enough above the range dock.
11. Transition to mist with a clean gap after the panel.
12. Use range title outside the panel in the light zone.
13. Keep the product row visible below the dock, not hidden beyond the fold.
14. On desktop, increase panel side padding but reduce vertical padding.
15. On mobile, avoid full panel framing. It becomes a cramped card.
16. On mobile, use a soft inset mist band instead of a glass card.
17. Keep text contrast high inside the panel.
18. Use the same category control treatment as the other variants for comparison.
19. Avoid a second dark category dock after a dark glass panel.
20. Recommended use: good for structure, weaker for emotional blend.

## R4 - Editorial Bridge and Emerging Range

1. Keep the editorial simplicity.
2. Keep the dark-to-light handoff, but soften it with the same ocean and fog blend.
3. Do not use a hard horizontal category slab.
4. Move the category dock into a translucent mist band.
5. Put the range title before products with enough space around it.
6. Let the products emerge from the blue fog, not from a flat cream block.
7. Give the product group a stable baseline.
8. Add carousel arrows as product controls, not hero controls.
9. Keep proof facts as a small rail under CTAs.
10. Avoid making proof and categories the same color and size.
11. Keep the hero wordmark slightly larger than R2.
12. Reduce top nav scale a touch to make the central brand feel calmer.
13. Add a subtle moonpath highlight behind the center product.
14. Use category separators sparingly.
15. On mobile, order should be hero, CTA, proof, title, category, product.
16. On mobile, prevent the category row from wrapping awkwardly.
17. On mobile, use short labels and icons.
18. Keep product packshots larger on desktop than current R4.
19. Avoid edge-to-edge cream because it loses the MapleMoon atmosphere.
20. Recommended use: best transition logic to merge into the R2 recommendation.
