## Do not roll out blindly

- The FAQ search card, result count, numbered accordion and support panel are FAQ-specific.
- The pale-blue wash must not override approved product photography, the moonlit homepage hero, or Carob Story’s paper-like surfaces.
- Never spread a "pending" treatment to confirmed prices, product availability, stockist counts, testimonials or policy language.
