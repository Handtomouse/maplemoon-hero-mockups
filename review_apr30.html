How to read these

These are static high-fidelity comps, not the live site. Each direction is internally consistent in colour, type, and rhythm; different directions on the same page mean we ship a different brand voice.

Per scope: 3 homepage variants (A, B, C), 2 collection variants (A, C), 2 product variants (A, C). Direction B inherits A's collection and product pages so we are not asking you to choose between three flavours of every page; we are asking which voice is right.
