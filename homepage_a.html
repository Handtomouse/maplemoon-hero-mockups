The pod that doesn't need to pretend.

Carob is a legume pod, native to the Mediterranean. Roasted and ground, it's naturally sweet without added sugar. No caffeine. No theobromine. No bitterness to engineer around.

We grow ours down the road. Source it. Roast it. Press it with cacao butter into bars and crescents. That's the whole process.
