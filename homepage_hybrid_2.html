What is carob?

Carob is a naturally sweet pod that grows in the Mediterranean sun. No caffeine, no bitterness, no need for added sugar.

We slow-roast it in Brunswick Heads and turn it into smooth bars with simple, real ingredients. The result is gentle, grounding and good for you.
