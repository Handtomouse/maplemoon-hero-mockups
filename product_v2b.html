The Story

The Original Pure Carob bar is MapleMoon in its simplest form. Australian-grown carob, slow-roasted and milled, combined with organic cacao butter and hand-tempered in our Byron Bay kitchen. There's nothing added because nothing needs to be.

Carob is naturally sweet, rich in flavour, and entirely free from caffeine. Each bar is wrapped by hand and made in small batches.

If you've never tried carob before, start here.

What's Inside

Australian carob. Organic cacao butter. That's the entire list. No sugar, no sweetener, no flavouring, no additives.
