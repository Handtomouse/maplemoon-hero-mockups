Our Story

Carob Isn't Trying
to Be Chocolate

It's its own thing. Naturally sweet, caffeine-free, and grown right here in Australia. We started Maple Moon in Byron Bay because we believed carob deserved better than the back shelf. Better ingredients, better craft, better packaging.

Every product is handmade in small batches. Organic Australian carob and cacao butter. No compound, no fillers, no shortcuts.
