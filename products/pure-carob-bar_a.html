Tasting

Honey-roasted, malt, the faintest dark fruit.

Carob roasted to the second crack reads like burnt honey on the front of the palate, with a malt note in the middle and a finish that turns slightly raisin. The cacao butter rounds the texture without softening the flavour.

It is sweet without sugar, structured without bitterness. If you have only had carob compounded with cocoa or sugar, this bar will reset what you thought it could do.

Materials

Three ingredients.
That's the whole list.

Why this bar

We wanted the bar that didn't exist.

Most carob you find at health-food shops is compounded — bulked with milk solids, sugar, vegetable fat, and additives that are there to make carob behave like cheap chocolate. We started Maple Moon because that bar wasn't a real category.

This is the one with the lowest ingredient count we ship. Everything else in the range is built on top of it.
