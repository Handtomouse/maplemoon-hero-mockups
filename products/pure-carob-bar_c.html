Tasting

Honey-roasted, malt, faint dark fruit.

Front of palate reads as burnt honey. Mid-palate is malt. Finish turns slightly raisin. Cacao butter rounds the texture without softening flavour.

Pairings

Espresso, oat milk, dry sherry.

The malt note plays well with anything roasted. Avoid overly sweet wines. A dry oloroso or amontillado is unusually good.

Storage

Cool, dry, away from direct light.

No fridge needed. Avoid above 24°C if possible — the cacao butter will bloom and the bar texture goes chalky. Bloom is harmless, just less elegant.
