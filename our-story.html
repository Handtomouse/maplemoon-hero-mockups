The people behind the product.

Carli & Dylan met in a health food store, a fitting beginning for two people who believe deeply that what we eat affects how we feel, physically and emotionally.

They began creating flavours that are rich, joyful, and deeply satisfying, an explosion of sensation without caffeine or guilt.

Maple Moon (affectionately known as "the vegan ultimate honeymooning experience") is a true reflection of who they are and what they value: nourishment, simplicity, love, and connection.

Born in Bondi but now residing in the Brunswick Heads, Northern Rivers.

Co-founder

Carli

After struggling with gut and digestive issues, Carli learned firsthand that what you put into your body can have a profound effect on how you feel. As a Montessori school teacher, she also saw this play out through her work with children, observing and studying the impact that food and nutrition can have on behaviour, growth and development.

Read Carli's story

While passionately working alongside children and families, Carli began eating carob to satisfy her afternoon and nighttime cravings for something rich and chocolatey, without the caffeine and heavy stimulants found in cacao. Cacao products often left her feeling jittery, wired and affected her sleep, which made her question the impact these stimulants could have on smaller, developing bodies.

Her love for carob began when she started experimenting with it at home. Her roommate loved the flavour, and together they decided to try selling it. Although they eventually went their separate ways with the idea, the seed had been planted. Carli couldn't let go of the vision and continued to develop the concept, always believing there was something special about carob and its potential as a delicious alternative to traditional chocolate.

From there, Maple Moon grew as more and more people discovered the flavour and fell in love with it.

Today, Carli is the researcher and creator behind Maple Moon's new flavours. Every ingredient is purposefully chosen, not only because it creates a delicious flavour combination, but because Carli believes in creating products made with thoughtfully selected ingredients that contribute to whole-body nourishment.

Her passion for health, nutrition and conscious eating is at the heart of everything she creates. From researching ingredients and developing new recipes to working on marketing, sales and sharing the story behind the brand, Carli is deeply involved in every part of the Maple Moon journey.

Carli is a dedicated Ashtanga yogi who practises every morning. She loves spending time by the ocean, especially during whale season, when she can often be found on the beach watching for her favourite animals as they make their way along the coast.

Co-founder

Dylan

Dylan has always been a natural athlete, whether it was running, soccer or surfing. His passion for sport led him to explore how to optimise his health by understanding the body and what it needs to perform at its best. This sparked a deeper interest in nutrition, nourishment, rest and recovery, and the role they all play in supporting the body and achieving peak performance.

Read Dylan's story

When Dylan met Carli, she shared her idea of creating and selling her own carob treats. In the early days of their relationship, they jokingly called their adventures "Maple Mooning" instead of honeymooning, as Dylan was a strict vegan at the time and didn't eat honey. Although Carli had planned to move to Byron Bay and Dylan intended to settle in Sydney, their connection, love and shared passion for health, nature and conscious living kept bringing them together. Just six months into their relationship, they decided to take a leap and start Maple Moon as a passion project, with a shared vision of creating delicious, guilt-free treats that people could genuinely feel good about eating.

While Carli was the creator and flavour visionary behind the products, Dylan became the driving force behind turning the idea into a real business. He taught himself how to technically produce carob bars to perfection and, importantly, how to scale production as demand grew. From there, he took on the logistics and operations of the business, from managing the website to building relationships with stockists and distributors and ensuring the finished products made their way onto shelves across Australia.

Today, Dylan is the magic behind the scenes, managing the operations that keep Maple Moon moving. From sourcing the highest-quality raw ingredients to overseeing production, logistics and getting the final products into the hands of customers and onto store shelves, he is involved in every step of the journey.

Like Carli, Dylan has a deep love for the ocean and spends as much time as possible in the water. Rain, hail or shine, if the waves are good, you can almost always find him out surfing.

How Maple Moon began.

Maple Moon began simply, with a craving. Our co-founder Carli was searching for a sweet evening treat that felt good in her body. Something indulgent, comforting, and grounding, without the buzz or stimulation of caffeine.

After making her own raw cacao creations, she began noticing something wasn't right. Even in the evenings, she felt jittery, wired, and restless. That's when she realised cacao naturally contains caffeine, and because of its bitterness, it's often paired with heavily processed sweeteners.

That's when she turned to carob. Naturally sweet. Caffeine-free. Gentle and grounding.

But there was a problem, no carob products on the market met her standards. Most contained lectins, stabilisers, or were overly processed. So she stripped it all back and made her own.
