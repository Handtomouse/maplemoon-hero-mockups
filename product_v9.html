Maple Moon Pure Carob Bar

Where does it start?

The Pod

Every bar begins as a carob pod, grown in the sun-drenched orchards of Australia. The pods are harvested at peak ripeness, when the natural sugars have fully developed. No fertilisers, no pesticides. Just time, sun, and soil.

How is it made?

The Craft

Slow-roasted and milled into a fine powder, the carob is combined with organic cacao butter and hand-tempered in our Byron Bay kitchen. Each batch is small. Each bar is set and wrapped by hand. There are no shortcuts.

Ingredients: Australian carob, organic cacao butter. Nothing else.
