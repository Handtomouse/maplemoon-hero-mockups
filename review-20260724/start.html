Maple Moon · Local design review

Website walkthrough

A guided look through the current website direction. The aim is to agree on what feels right visually and capture the remaining information needed to finish the site accurately.

What we are reviewing

The page flow, imagery, tone, hierarchy, and how the product range and story feel together. This is a working local version, not the final live store.

What we will park

Pricing and purchase links, policies, testimonials, final founder photos, verified stockist details, social links, and Shopify setup are kept separate until confirmed.

Walk through the pages

01First impression

Homepage

Start with the brand atmosphere, product range, and the rhythm of the page as it unfolds.

  • Does this feel like Maple Moon from the first screen?
  • Is the product range easy to understand without feeling too busy?
  • Which visual moments should stay, soften, or change?

Waiting on you: customer testimonials, with the names we are allowed to publish.

Open homepage
02Range and shopping

Shop

Look at how the range is scanned, how the sampler reads, and whether the hierarchy makes sense before final purchase wiring.

  • Does the product range feel easy to browse?
  • Are the featured products and pending formats communicated clearly?
  • What should feel most prominent here?

Waiting on you: confirm the retail prices shown are correct and current.

Open Shop
03People and place

Our Story

Review the tone of the makers story, the imagery, and the balance of personal history with the product story.

  • Does this sound and feel like your story?
  • Does the current imagery point in the right direction for founder photos?
  • Is there anything essential we have not made room for?

Waiting on you: founder photos, a short bio each, and your favourite way to enjoy.

Open Our Story
04Education

What is Carob?

Review how clearly the page explains the ingredient and how the pod-to-bar process is shown.

  • Is the explanation clear to someone new to carob?
  • Does the comparison feel calm, readable, and true to your product?
  • Does the making process feel credible and interesting?
Open Carob Story
05Finding Maple Moon

Stockists

Review the finder experience and map-style visual treatment. It is deliberately a non-live preview until verified store data is supplied.

  • Does this feel useful and on-brand?
  • Which store information matters most when the final data is ready?
  • Should the map remain a supporting visual, or become more prominent?

Waiting on you: the verified stockist list, and which details we may publish.

Open Stockists
06Support

FAQ

Review the order and clarity of the questions, while public policy details remain pending confirmation.

  • Are the first questions the ones customers will actually ask?
  • Does the page feel reassuring and easy to scan?
  • What support topics should be added once wording is confirmed?

Waiting on you: approved shipping, returns and help wording.
