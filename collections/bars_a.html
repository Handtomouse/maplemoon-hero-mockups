The range

Carob bars,
six flavours.

Roasted slow, pressed by hand. Three or four ingredients per bar. No emulsifier, no compound, no shortcuts. Pick the one that does the job, or take the box.

Pure Carob $10 Three ingredients. The flagship. Golden Coconut $11 Toasted coconut folded through. Peppermint $11 Cold-pressed peppermint, sharp finish. Hazelnut $12 Roasted hazelnut paste, the familiar flavour. Chilli $12 Slow chilli warmth. Finishes hot. Almond $12 Whole almond inclusions, generously.

Material

Carob is a legume. The pod is naturally sweet.

No caffeine, no theobromine, no engineering around bitterness. We grow ours 20 minutes from the kitchen, in northern New South Wales. Pressed with cacao butter, scored, packed by hand. Same recipe since 2024.
