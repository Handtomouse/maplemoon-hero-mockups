Standards

Naturally sweet. No added sugar.

Carob is sweet because it's a legume pod. We don't add cane, coconut, rice malt, or any other sugar. The sweetness is the pod.

No

Caffeine, theobromine, emulsifier, vegetable oil.

Carob has none of the alkaloids cacao does. We don't compound, we don't extend with oil, we don't smooth the texture with lecithin.

Certifications

Organic. Vegan. Gluten free.

Certified across the range. Hazelnut and Almond are made in a facility that handles tree nuts.
